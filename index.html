FAQ

Common questions before we talk

What does a scoped evaluation involve?

We agree on the scope, timing, supported tax period, and success criteria together. With hands-on setup and mapping support, your team evaluates linked Excel workpapers and a tie-out report alongside its existing process. We review the results together before deciding on any next step.

Do we need to replace our existing provision software?

No replacement is required for an initial evaluation. We agree on a specific workflow to test alongside your existing process. The scope is confirmed before any work begins.

Do you replace our CPA firm or auditor?

No. Polaris supports provision preparation and documentation. Your team's review procedures, your advisors' responsibilities, and the auditor's evaluation remain in place.

How do you handle our data and confidentiality?

Before you share company data, we agree on confidentiality, permitted use, access, and retention. An initial conversation can stay at the process level without sharing confidential workpapers.

Will automation replace our reviewer or preparer?

No. The point is to cut the manual preparation so the same people spend more time on review, judgment, and planning. Polaris supports your team, it doesn’t replace them.

What if we’re not sure we’re a fit?

Start with a conversation about what works well in your provision process and what could improve. If there is a fit, we can discuss a walkthrough, a scoped evaluation, or consulting support.
