FAQ

Common questions before we talk

What does a Polaris pilot involve?

We start with your current provision process, map where the manual, error-prone work is, and work alongside your team to make it faster and more review-ready. It runs as a defined pilot scope before any longer-term rollout.

Do we need a tax specialist on staff?

You don’t need a dedicated tax specialist on staff to use Polaris, and Polaris doesn’t replace one either. It brings structure and senior perspective to preparing the provision, but technical positions and ASC 740 sign-off stay with your team and your advisors.

Do you replace our CPA firm or auditor?

No. Polaris improves the process, data, and documentation that feed your existing advisors and audit, which tends to make their work smoother, not redundant.

How do you handle our data and confidentiality?

We take confidentiality seriously. Before any work begins, we document how pilot data is handled and agree the appropriate confidentiality and access controls. You always know what data is used and why.

Will automation replace our reviewer or preparer?

No. The point is to cut the manual preparation so the same people spend more time on review, judgment, and planning. Polaris supports your team, it doesn’t replace them.

What if we’re not sure we’re a fit?

That’s a fine place to start. A short conversation will tell us whether the provision is where we can help right now, or whether it isn’t the right time yet.
