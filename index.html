Meet Polly

From your trial balance to workpapers you can review.

Polly handles repeatable preparation within your tax provision process: calculations, supporting schedules, and checks. It generates Excel workpapers for your team and advisers to review, rather than replacing their review.

01   Set up

Map your accounts and entities

Prepare the trial balance and supporting tax data. Review how accounts feed the provision before running calculations.

02   Calculate

Generate workpapers in minutes

Run supported calculations and produce formula-linked Excel schedules. When inputs change, update them and rerun.

03   Review

Trace the numbers and check the totals

Follow formulas to supporting inputs. Use the tie-out report to see which checks passed and which exceptions need investigation.

Calculation checks before review

Polly independently recalculates the finished Excel workbook and compares the formula results against what its calculation engine computed. If the results disagree, the download is blocked. A tie-out report documents the checks and exceptions, helping your team catch calculation discrepancies before review.

Before we talk

A few practical questions.

What is agreed before we start?

We confirm your entities, tax period and applicable scenarios, along with setup, training, support, access and deliverables. Current calculation rules are designed for 2026. Custom work is separately scoped and is not included automatically.

What happens when our inputs change?

New accounts or changed tax treatments require review and updates to mappings or inputs before rerunning. Polly generates its own workpapers; it does not automatically update every existing workbook.

How much time will Polly save our team?

Supported calculations run in minutes once inputs and mappings are ready. Total preparation and review time depends on your data, complexity and existing process. An evaluation can compare the work involved; we do not promise a fixed percentage saving.

Does Polly replace our tax team or advisers?

No. Polly supports the preparation work, not the replacement of your team, CPA firm, or auditor. Your team and advisers retain their respective responsibilities for tax treatments, inputs, review, and sign-off. Validation checks identify specific inconsistencies; they do not establish that the entire provision is correct.

Can we test against a prior-year provision?

We first agree on the period and supported scope. Polly currently uses 2026 tax rules, so prior-year data may produce differences caused by law changes. Those differences need to be addressed in the evaluation plan before comparison.

How is Polly hosted, and how would we share data?

Polly currently runs locally for demonstrations and pilot work; it is not yet a fully hosted SaaS offering. We can begin with fictional data. Before company data is shared, we agree on an approved environment, confidentiality, access, permitted use and retention with your team.

What if our existing tools already do this?

Start with the preparation your current tools already handle well and what remains manual. The conversation is about a specific preparation workflow, not replacing your entire provision system. Polly is worth evaluating only if it addresses a meaningful gap.
