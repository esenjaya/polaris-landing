How the Excel formulas connect the totals

In this sample, the summary adds U.S. current tax of $4,496,972 in cell C10 to foreign current tax of $2,168,053 in cell D10. Cell E10 calculates the total of $6,665,025:

=C10+D10

The supporting schedule totals the entity-level calculations separately. The tie-out check compares that amount with the summary total and shows a $0 difference.

Your reviewer can follow these formulas in Excel and check that the schedules agree. Reviewing the inputs and tax treatment remains your team's responsibility.

Before we talk

A few practical questions

What does my team need to get started?

We first confirm your entities, tax period, and scope. Setup includes account and entity mappings, a trial balance, and supporting tax data. We work through the specific requirements with your team before beginning. As your data changes, new accounts or changes in classification or tax treatment need review and any necessary mapping updates.

Can Polly work alongside our existing provision process?

We start with what your current tools handle well and where manual preparation remains. Polly can be evaluated for an agreed part of the workflow without committing to replace your existing system. We confirm how its inputs and outputs would fit your process.
